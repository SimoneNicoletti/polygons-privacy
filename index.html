Privacy Policy — Polygons

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Polygons ("the App") does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising purposes.

Data we collect

  1. Game Center (Apple): if you sign in with your Game Center account, the App displays your player name/alias and avatar, and stores your leaderboard scores and unlocked achievements. This data is handled by Apple in accordance with the Apple Privacy Policy.
  2. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics, Google): if the App crashes, it may send anonymous technical data (device model, OS version, error logs) to improve stability. No data that directly identifies you is collected for this purpose. This data is processed in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.
  3. Local guest identifier: if you play as a "guest", the App generates a random identifier stored only on your device, used solely to keep your progress.

Purpose of processing

Data is used solely to provide the game's features (leaderboards, achievements, saving progress) and for the correct operation of the App.

Retention and your rights

Local data remains on your device and can be removed by uninstalling the App. Game Center data can be managed from your device Settings or your Apple account. You may contact us at polygons.aws@gmail.com with any privacy-related request.

Children

The App is not intended for children under 4 years of age and does not knowingly collect personal data from minors.
